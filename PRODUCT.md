## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS (user choice). Motion via GSAP + ScrollTrigger + Lenis from CDN. No build step.

## Users

Women in Brasília–DF, mostly arriving from Instagram (@dradayannemoura) on a phone, who want healthier, cleaner skin and are deciding whether to book an evaluation. Secondary: returning clients checking prices and packages.

## Product Purpose

Website for Dra. Dayanne Moura, Estética Facial e Corporal, specialised in limpeza de pele. The site explains the treatments, shows prices and packages, builds trust, and moves the visitor to book an evaluation on WhatsApp. Success = WhatsApp conversations started.

## Positioning

Skin-first philosophy: "a limpeza é a base". Her content argues that expensive skincare is useless on skin that has not been cleaned, and that every treatment starts with an evaluation. Tagline from her bio: "A beleza de hoje é ser leve, real e do seu jeito".

## Operating Context

Booking happens only on WhatsApp (+55 61 8429-8360, wa.me/556184298360). Instagram is the main acquisition channel. Payment up to 5x sem juros on packages.
